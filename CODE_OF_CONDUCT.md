# Code of conduct

WeldCheck welcomes respectful contributions from people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient and specific.
- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's records, photographs or contact details.
- Accept reasonable review and correction.
- Keep technical and safety claims evidence-based.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, impersonation and publishing private information without permission are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that breach these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact and repeated behavior.

Report serious conduct concerns privately using the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/OLAOLEX). For platform abuse or urgent safety concerns, use GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
