Step 3 of 3 · Weld photo

Upload the finished weld

Take a clear smartphone photo of the cooled, cleaned weld bead and upload it for the appearance check.

For a reliable check: good light on the bead, a steady hand about 15 to 20 cm away, a plain background, and the weld filling most of the frame.

The photo stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
