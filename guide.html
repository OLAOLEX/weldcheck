Help and user guide

Use WeldCheck with confidence

Follow the guided steps, use a clear photograph, then review and confirm the result. The words on this page match the words used in the app.

1. Enter the job details

Use one sample number for each workpiece. Record the joint, thickness, electrode, welding position, current and operator.

  • Material is fixed as mild steel for this project.
  • Use the note field for short information that is not covered elsewhere.
  • You can return to an unfinished job later.

2. Complete the checklist

Mark every preparation item as Done or Pending. All nine must be Done before the photo step opens.

If something is pending: correct it first, then return and mark it Done.

3. Take a useful photograph

  • Clean the weld area and use even light.
  • Keep the whole bead and both weld toes in view.
  • Hold the camera steady and avoid glare.
  • Do not use a distant photo or heavy digital zoom.

WeldCheck checks brightness, contrast, sharpness and size before the photograph is sent for inspection.

4. Read the result

Acceptable
No allowed visible issue was found in this photograph.
Visible issues
One or more visible conditions are shown. Read the observations and next actions.
Retake photo
The image is not clear enough. Follow the photo advice and try again.

Possible reasons are suggestions, not confirmed causes.

5. Confirm what you observed

Choose Result matches, Correct conditions, or Not sure. Your choice is saved separately and never changes the original WeldCheck result.

A supervisor can optionally add a reference assessment for project evaluation.

Reports and downloads

  • Print or save PDF creates a readable inspection report.
  • Download spreadsheet creates a CSV file for tables and analysis.
  • Download full backup creates a JSON safety copy without photographs. Keep it private.

Account, privacy and common questions

Can I start without a Google account?

Yes. Continue as a guest. Your guest workspace is private, and you can connect it to Google later to use it across devices.

Does WeldCheck certify a weld?

No. It describes visible surface appearance from one photograph. It cannot confirm internal condition, strength, penetration, safety or welding-code compliance.

Why was my photo rejected?

It may be too dark, too bright, blurred, low resolution or poorly framed. The app lists the specific problems and tells you what to change.

Does a recheck replace the old result?

No. Every recheck creates a new inspection in the job timeline so the history remains clear.

Where are my photographs stored?

Cloud photographs are private and protected by your account. Only short-lived secure links are used when a photograph is displayed.

Remember: WeldCheck supports learning, record keeping and visible-surface review. A qualified person and suitable tests are still needed for safety-critical decisions.
