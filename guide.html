Help and user guide

Use WeldCheck for repeated practice

Follow an approved exercise, check the actual setup, record the visible result, then plan the next attempt.

1. Choose the exercise

Select the exercise supplied by your workshop. Its material, joint, plate range, electrode, position, current range and preparation checks come from the approved template.

No exercise shown? Ask the administrator to add a supervisor-approved template. WeldCheck does not invent welding values.

2. Record the actual setup

Enter what is actually on the bench and machine. WeldCheck names every mismatch and shows the approved requirement.

  • Correct setup items before welding.
  • Complete each exercise-specific preparation check.
  • “Ready” confirms the recorded rules only; it does not predict weld quality.

3. Weld and photograph

  • Follow workshop safety rules and supervisor instructions.
  • Let the sample cool and clean loose slag.
  • Use even light and keep the bead and both edges in view.
  • Retake dark, blurred or poorly framed photographs.

4. Review visible evidence

The AI may return acceptable appearance, visible issues, or retake photo. It describes supported visible conditions only.

Setup guidance is produced separately from approved rules. A photograph is not used to claim that current, travel speed, angle or another factor caused the result.

Plan another attempt

Confirm or correct the visible conditions, save what you will check next, then start another attempt. The comparison shows setup changes and which conditions were resolved, remain, or are new.

Supervisor review and reports

A supervisor can record accepted, another attempt needed, or incomplete, with an optional name and comment. Print the attempt report or download CSV/JSON records from History.

Common questions

Does “Ready” mean the weld will pass?

No. It only means the recorded setup and preparation match the selected exercise.

Does WeldCheck certify a weld?

No. It cannot confirm internal condition, penetration, strength, safety or code compliance.

Does a new attempt erase the old one?

No. Every attempt and photograph stays in the exercise timeline.

Can I use it without Google?

Yes. Continue as a private guest and connect Google later to keep records across devices.

Remember: WeldCheck supports structured learning and visible-surface review. Follow workshop procedures and use qualified review and suitable tests for safety-critical decisions.
