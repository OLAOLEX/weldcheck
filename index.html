What WeldCheck adds

It turns scattered setup notes and a weld photograph into one traceable work record.

Pre-welding control
For a full job, compares the actual setup with the entered work requirement.
Visible evidence
Records supported surface conditions without claiming internal quality.
Context-aware action
Uses the workflow and recorded setup to frame useful next checks.
Traceable history
Keeps each inspection, correction, review and report without overwriting.

Important scope

WeldCheck assesses visible surface appearance from one photograph. It does not determine internal weld condition, penetration, mechanical strength or welding-code compliance.
