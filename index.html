How it works

1
Create a job
Record the sample number, material, electrode size, and a short note.
2
Complete the pre-weld checklist
Confirm each preparation step before the arc is started.
3
Upload a clear weld photo
Capture the finished bead with your phone camera after welding.
4
Get the appearance result
The system groups the image into one visual category and saves the record.

Weld appearance categories

Every checked image is grouped into one of four simple categories.

Good weld appearance
The bead looks regular and clean.
Visible holes and rough bead
Holes, rough surface, and irregular finishing.
Excessive spatter and uneven bead
Heavy spatter, uneven width, and rough surface.
Unclear image
Blurred, too dark, too bright, or poorly framed.
